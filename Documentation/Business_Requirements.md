# Business Requirements

## Objective

Build a Snowflake Data Warehouse that provides insights into global energy production, consumption, and greenhouse gas emissions.

---

## Business Questions

### Energy Consumption

- Which countries consume the most energy?
- How has energy consumption changed over time?
- Which energy source is growing fastest?

---

### Electricity

- Which countries generate the most electricity?
- Compare electricity generation vs demand.

---

### Renewable Energy

- Which countries use the highest renewable energy?
- Growth trend of renewable energy.

---

### Carbon Emissions

- Which countries emit the highest greenhouse gases?
- Relationship between electricity generation and emissions.

---

### Population & GDP

- Compare GDP with energy consumption.
- Compare population with electricity demand.

---

## Technical Requirements

- Snowflake Database
- Automated Data Loading
- Data Cleaning
- Views
- Analysis Queries
- Window Functions
- Streams
- Tasks
- Dashboard
